# e4b-mean — mean and weighted mean reducer (`sf.Mean`, `mean@1`, `weighted_mean@1`)

## Intent

E4b unit 3 of 7. Add the deterministic mean and weighted mean of the members' numbers (scenarios
S3 and S4 of the E4b spec, ans:Q1, ans:Q2, ans:Q3, ans:Q9). The Engine gets `mean@1` and
`weighted_mean@1`: exact `Decimal` sums and one division rounded to 34 significant digits (P1). A
member with no usable weight is dropped and is never the `nearest@1` pick
(`Reduction.participants`, P9). The SDK gets `sf.Mean` and the block `sf.Reduce("mean")`, the
reducer name `mean`, and the optional evidence field `mean` (P8). No new failure code, no new schema
id (ans:Q5, P7).
