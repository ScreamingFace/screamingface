# e14-sdk-replay — `evaluate(replay=...)`, local signing keys, `publish_cache_version` (E14, OME-1307)

## Intent

`evaluate(..., replay=pin)` (sync and async, and the `sf.evaluate` facade) checks the pin grammar and the one-Candidate
rule, asks the Scoreboard for a replay grant (`POST /v1/replay-grants`, C6) before any run, carries the grant opaquely on
the engine start request as `X-SF-Cache-Replay` (C1), reads the root replay counters of the engine cache-summary frame
(C12), and puts a `replay` block in the report and in the C4 submit payload. `publish_cache_version(result_id)` (C10)
is added to both twins and the facade. `screamingface up` makes the local Ed25519 signing keys once and sets the signer
and verifier environment of the local gateway and scoreboard before they start (RP-20). Plan:
`docs/plan/2026-09-29-e14-reproducible-submission/SDK-replay.md`. Spec: `docs/spec/2026-09-29-e14-reproducible-submission/`
(RP-16..RP-20, PB-21; C1, C4 `replay`, C6, C10, C12).

## Contract checks (plan section 2.1, before GREEN of rows 9, 10 and 17)

Scoreboard pin parser (SB-registry, on this branch): `apps/scoreboard/src/scoreboard/core/registry/pins.py:22-51`
(`parse_pin`), `_REVISION_RE` at `:18` (`^r([1-9][0-9]{0,8})$`), `_DATE_RE` at `:19`, the naive-time refusal at `:48-50`;
name rule `apps/scoreboard/src/scoreboard/core/registry/names.py:14` (`_NAME_RE`) and `:20-45` (`normalize_system_name`:
ASCII check BEFORE `lower()`, `fullmatch`, no strip). The `result:` / `score:` forms are SB-grants' (not yet merged).

The plan text section 4.1 differs from the scoreboard in three edge cases:
1. revision digits: plan `r([1-9][0-9]*)`, scoreboard at most 9 digits (`{0,8}`);
2. name: plan `name.lower()` then `^...$`, scoreboard requires `isascii()` BEFORE `lower()` (KELVIN SIGN would become
   ASCII `k`) and uses `fullmatch` (`$` also matches before a trailing newline);
3. date: plan "suffix of length 10 -> `date.fromisoformat`" would take ISO week dates such as `2026-W01-1`; the
   scoreboard takes a date only for `\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}`.
The plan invariant is "the two parsers must accept and refuse the same forms". The SDK parser mirrors the scoreboard
parser in these three points (see Deviations). The plan row 9 and 10 cases are not affected.
