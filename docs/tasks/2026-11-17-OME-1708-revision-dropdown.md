A revision selector on each Benchmark leaderboard: defaults to "Latest", lists earlier
revisions with readable labels, and scopes the ranked table, frontier and submission
history to the chosen revision. HTML mockups for product feedback come first.

OME-1909 depends on it: its re-grade flow bumps revisions and shows them through this
dropdown.
